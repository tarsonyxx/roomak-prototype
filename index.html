Before you get started

Welcome to RooMak, a prototype for exploring room information and scheduling at the University of Makati. Please take a moment to review the following before continuing.

ⓘ

This is a prototype

Features and information are provided for demonstration and evaluation. Some functions may be incomplete, simulated, or subject to change.

↻

Your actions may not be saved

Room requests, issue reports, and other interactions may not be stored or forwarded to university staff. Do not rely on RooMak for actual room bookings or urgent reports.

▣

A laptop is recommended

RooMak can be opened on mobile devices, but a laptop or desktop is recommended for the best experience, particularly when exploring the interactive floor plan and its controls.

◆

Protect your information

Avoid entering real passwords, sensitive personal information, or confidential university data into this prototype.
