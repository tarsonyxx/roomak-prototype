PRE-ENTRY BRIEFING · PROTOTYPE PREVIEW

Before you get started.

Welcome to RooMak, a student research prototype for exploring room availability and scheduling at the University of Makati. Review these four notes before proceeding to sign in.

01

This is a prototype

Features and room schedules are provided for demonstration and evaluation. Some functions may be simulated or subject to change.

02

Actions stay local

Room requests and issue reports are saved only in your browser and are never sent to university staff.

03

Laptop recommended

Accessible on mobile devices, though a laptop or desktop gives the best view of the interactive floor plan.

04

Protect your info

Avoid entering real passwords, sensitive personal details, or confidential university data.
